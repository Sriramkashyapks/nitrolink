## The Idea

**Enter your ENS name. Get a payment profile. Share it.**

Your friends visit your page, connect their wallet, and send you money in *any token, on any chain*. We handle the complexity—swaps, bridges, settlement. You receive USDC on Arc.

No intermediaries. No waiting. Just payments.
